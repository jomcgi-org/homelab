# Dieter Rams

German industrial designer. Joined Braun in 1955 and ran its design department from 1961 to 1995. Designed furniture for Vitsœ and wrote the ten principles of good design.

## Key works
- **SK 4 radio-phonograph** (1956, Braun, with Hans Gugelot): a clear acrylic lid instead of a wooden cabinet. People called it "Snow White's Coffin". [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dieter_Rams)
- **606 Universal Shelving System** (1960, Vitsœ): a modular system that is still in production. [Metropolis](https://metropolismag.com/programs/dieters-top-ten/)
- **TP 1 radio/phono** (1959, Braun): a portable radio and record player. [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/gb/about/good-design)
- **620 Chair Programme** (1962, Vitsœ): modular seating. [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/dieter-rams)
- **T 1000 world receiver** (1963, Braun): Vitsœ uses it to illustrate "unobtrusive". [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/gb/about/good-design)
- **MPZ 21 citrus juicer** (1972, Braun, with Jürgen Greubel): Vitsœ uses it to illustrate "useful". [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/gb/about/good-design)

## Ideas
- **Ten principles.** Good design is innovative, useful, aesthetic, understandable, unobtrusive, honest, long-lasting and thorough down to the last detail. It is also environmentally friendly and uses as little design as possible. [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/gb/about/good-design)
- **Where they came from.** In the 1970s Rams was worried about how chaotic the made world was becoming, and he saw his own work as part of that. He asked himself whether his design was good design, and the principles were his answer. [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/gb/about/good-design)
- **Not fixed.** Rams has said the principles can't be final, because technology and culture keep changing. [Metropolis](https://metropolismag.com/programs/dieters-top-ten/)
- **Obsolescence.** He argued in the 1970s that designing for obsolescence is a crime. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dieter_Rams)
- **Design is for people.** You can't understand good design without understanding people. From his 1976 Vitsœ speech in New York. [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/dieter-rams-speech)
