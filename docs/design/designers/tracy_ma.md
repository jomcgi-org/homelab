# Tracy Ma

Graphic designer born in Hong Kong and raised in Toronto. [Booklyn](https://booklyn.org/artists/tracy-ma) Known for the strange, collage-heavy covers she made for Bloomberg Businessweek, and later for visual editing at The New York Times. [SSENSE](https://www.ssense.com/en-us/editorial/design/graphic-times-with-new-york-times-designer-tracy-ma), [tracyma.com](https://www.tracyma.com/)

## Timeline
- **2010**: graduated in design from York University, Toronto. [Booklyn](https://booklyn.org/artists/tracy-ma)
- **2011–2016**: Bloomberg Businessweek. Started on a three-month stint and stayed five years, rising to deputy creative director and working on more than 200 issues. Worked under Richard Turley. [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/tracy-ma-graphic-design-printed-pages-aw18-041218)
- **2016**: creative director at Matter Studios, then freelance work and teaching at Parsons. [AIGA Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/tracy-mas-giant-menu-for-an-upscale-restaurant-was-deliberately-too-big-to-read-an-elbow-knocking-affair/)
- **2018–2021**: The New York Times, visual editor on the Styles desk. [tracyma.com](https://www.tracyma.com/)
- **2021–**: graphic design lead at Homer, Frank Ocean's brand. Her site hasn't been updated since then, so this is unconfirmed. [tracyma.com](https://www.tracyma.com/)
- **Clients**: Christine and the Queens, Columbia GSAPP, Google Creative Labs, Medium, Breaker Magazine, SSENSE. [tracyma.com](https://www.tracyma.com/)

## Key works
- **"Guess who gets to decide how much everything costs?"** (Feb 2016, Businessweek): a baby covered in price stickers that spell out the headline. [SSENSE](https://www.ssense.com/en-us/editorial/design/graphic-times-with-new-york-times-designer-tracy-ma)
- **The How To Issue, The Interview Issue, "The New Republic of Porn"** (2014, Businessweek): issue packages and spreads. [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/tracy-ma-bloomberg-catch-up)
- **Businessweek Design 2016**: creative direction, set design and signage for the magazine's live event. [Parsons](https://amt.parsons.edu/?p=33771)
- **Total Power Move** (Matter Studios): design and art direction for the studio's magazine. [Parsons](https://amt.parsons.edu/?p=33771)
- **Homer "A–OK" catalog** (2021, Homer): a 160-page catalog with creative direction by Frank Ocean. She did the layout and type. [tracyma.com](https://www.tracyma.com/)
- **Dream Chairs** (2021, SSENSE): sketches, design and 3D models. [tracyma.com](https://www.tracyma.com/)
- **The Attack On Voting** (2020, NYT Magazine): print and digital cover. [tracyma.com](https://www.tracyma.com/)
- **Election Distractor, What Is Celebrity?, Gen X Is A Mess** (2019–20, NYT): interactive pieces she designed and coded. [tracyma.com](https://www.tracyma.com/)
- **Menu for Smith**: a menu made deliberately too big to read comfortably at the table. [AIGA Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/tracy-mas-giant-menu-for-an-upscale-restaurant-was-deliberately-too-big-to-read-an-elbow-knocking-affair/)
- **The Reference Manual for Boredom and Distraction; Hong Kong Tapestries**: student projects from York. [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/bloomberg-tracy-ma)

## Ideas
- **Visual codes.** Her aim is to gather and use as many codes of visual culture as she can. [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/tracy-ma-graphic-design-international-womens-day-080317)
- **Power as subject.** Her work keeps asking who holds power, and how one image can take them down a notch. [SSENSE](https://www.ssense.com/en-us/editorial/design/graphic-times-with-new-york-times-designer-tracy-ma)
- **Tension is productive.** She says the friction at Bloomberg is what produced Businessweek's look. [SSENSE](https://www.ssense.com/en-us/editorial/design/graphic-times-with-new-york-times-designer-tracy-ma)
- **Garbage design under deadline.** When a news story had to be turned around in a day, the team used deliberately crude graphics to make it interesting. [Semi Permanent](https://semipermanent.com/stories/tracy-ma)
- **The in-joke.** She sees graphic design as finding common ground with the reader, often through a wink or an Easter egg. [Salon/Imprint](https://www.salon.com/2012/04/10/sketching_an_unfamiliar_world)
- **Photo research.** Digging through archives for found imagery is central to how she works. [Semi Permanent](https://semipermanent.com/stories/tracy-ma)
