# Caroline Walker

Scottish figurative painter, born 1982 in Dunfermline. [Stephen Friedman](https://www.stephenfriedman.com/artists/81-caroline-walker/biography/) She paints women at work and at home, often seen through windows or doorways. [Ocula](https://ocula.com/artists/caroline-walker/grimm-gallery/)

## Timeline
- **2004**: BA in Painting, Glasgow School of Art. [Grimm](https://grimmgallery.com/artists/47-caroline-walker/)
- **2009**: MA in Painting, Royal College of Art. [Grimm](https://grimmgallery.com/artists/47-caroline-walker/)
- **2009–2022**: lived and worked in London, with a studio in north London. [Art Collection](https://artcollection.dcms.gov.uk/person/walker-caroline), [Apollo](https://apollo-magazine.com/in-the-studio-with-caroline-walker/)
- **2022**: moved back to Scotland and started the *Nurture* series. [Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/art/exhibitions-shows/caroline-walker-hepworth-wakefield)
- **Now**: lives and works in Dunfermline. [Stephen Friedman](https://www.stephenfriedman.com/artists/81-caroline-walker/biography/)

## Key works
- **Ward Round I and II** (2012): early paintings that address the subject directly. [Studio International](https://studiointernational.com/caroline-walker-interview-women-feminism-nail-bars)
- **Nail bar paintings** (from 2016): made after she noticed nail bars multiplying near her London studio. [It's Nice That](https://itsnicethat.com/articles/caroline-walker-art-040619)
- **Home** (Kettle's Yard): women seeking asylum in London, re-establishing ordinary life in their homes. [Varsity](https://www.varsity.co.uk/arts/15338)
- **Paintings of her mother**: her mother cooking, cleaning and gardening in the family house in Scotland. [Ocula](https://ocula.com/art-galleries/ingleby-gallery/artists/caroline-walker/)
- **Shaping** (2019): oil on panel, now in the Government Art Collection. [Art Collection](https://artcollection.dcms.gov.uk/person/walker-caroline)
- **Nearby** (2021, Grimm New York): women in shops and cafés near her north London studio. [Apollo](https://apollo-magazine.com/in-the-studio-with-caroline-walker/)
- **Sticker Dolly Dressing** (2024): from the *Nurture* series on early motherhood. [Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/art/exhibitions-shows/caroline-walker-hepworth-wakefield)
- **Nocturnes – Twilight**: print in the National Galleries of Scotland collection, on display at Modern One, Edinburgh. [NGS](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/art-and-artists/262240)

## Exhibitions
- **Women's Work** (MAC Birmingham): survey of unseen jobs such as tailors and chambermaids, with audio interviews of the women she painted. [MAC](https://macbirmingham.co.uk/exhibition-archive/caroline-walker-womens-work)
- **Mothering** (2025, Hepworth Wakefield; toured to Pallant House): her largest museum show to date. [Apollo](https://apollo-magazine.com/caroline-walker-interview/)

## Ideas
- **Women's work.** She paints work by women that usually goes unnoticed: caregiving and chores, and also surgery and biochemistry. [Apollo](https://apollo-magazine.com/caroline-walker-interview/)
- **The glimpse.** Women seen through windows and door frames, in places that are neither fully public nor fully private. [Ocula](https://ocula.com/artists/caroline-walker/grimm-gallery/)
- **Composition.** She says Dutch genre painting shapes how she thinks about composition. [Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/art/exhibitions-shows/caroline-walker-hepworth-wakefield)
- **Lineage.** Critics link her work to Dutch Golden Age interiors, Manet and Mary Cassatt, especially in light and colour. [Ocula](https://ocula.com/artists/caroline-walker/baldwin-contemporary/)
- **Method.** At first she hired models and staged them for photographs. She now paints from her own photographs and her own experience. [Ocula](https://ocula.com/artists/caroline-walker/baldwin-contemporary/), [Elephant](https://elephant.art/the-eyes-of-caroline-walker-its-different-when-its-a-woman-looking-at-women-13052022/)
