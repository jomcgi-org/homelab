Decision

Draw big diagrams in the browser with ELK

Three ways to draw an architecture with groups and fan-out. Only ELK kept every edge out of the groups, at no server cost.

The options

What each engine costs and where it falls short.

Inline renderer (mmdr)

Rust, in the Discord bridge's renderer.

  • 0.4 s and 17 MB per diagram
  • Image appears right in the chat
  • Edges cross groups and labels
Recommended

Browser with ELK

Mermaid 12 in an artifact page.

  • No edge through a node or group
  • Zoom, light and dark, live charts
  • A link to open, not an inline image

Headless Chrome service

Screenshots of artifact pages.

  • Best layout and an inline image
  • About 130 MB and 1.1 s per render
  • A browser to run and sandbox
